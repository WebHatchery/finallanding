# Manual Playtest — The Final Landing

Run after changes to agents, the campaign, or any screen. Play with touch or
mouse only first, then repeat the starred steps with the keyboard shortcuts.
Check at 1920×1080 and at the 1280×720 minimum window.

## First hour
- [ ] Title → New Colony: pick each landing site and difficulty; New seed changes the seed; Land starts the run.
- [ ] The five-step tutorial names visible controls; Skip and Begin both dismiss it and it does not return on Continue.
- [ ] Tap a survivor: the Mind tab shows a goal with its reason, a plan with the current step, a reasoning log and beliefs. Close and tapping empty ground both dismiss it.
- [ ] Build: place tents, a Mess Hall and a Solar Array. Blocked spots explain why at the pointer; Close disarms the tool. Survivors haul materials and build on their own.
- [ ] Colony overlay: change a work priority and a policy; survivors' choices shift within an in-game day.
- [ ] Research: only known technologies show; select waiting and available ones; Set as research focus; watch progress and a eureka toast.
- [ ] Discovery: gather a new species and see the first-find entry; keep gathering until a technology is inspired; check the native finds strip, inspired-card colours and a resource node's inspector before and after its species is found.
- [ ] Orchard: build a Native Orchard after Native Cultivation and confirm the inspector names the planted species.
- [ ] Speed: pause, 1×, 2×, 3× from the top bar ★ (Space, 1, 2, 3).

## Minds and relationships
- [ ] Watch a hungry survivor whose stores are empty: the reasoning log shows a failed plan followed by another way (forage, ask a friend).
- [ ] A creature raid: survivors who see it take cover or defend; others nearby are warned; alerts jump the camera to the threat.
- [ ] Relations overlay: tap a survivor to see their two-sided opinions; partners, friends and rivals are coloured.
- [ ] Chronicle: daily reports read as a story; filters and paging work; tapping a toast opens the chronicle.

## Long arc
- [ ] Expeditions: build the gate, call for volunteers, see who signs up, and read the report when they return.
- [ ] Act IV Council: projected support differs per future, with named champions and opponents; proposing holds the vote.
- [ ] Results: the epilogue, colony record, highlights and every survivor's fate; New Colony and Title Screen work.
- [ ] Menu saves and returns to the title; Continue restores the same colony and day.
